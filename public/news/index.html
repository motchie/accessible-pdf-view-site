What's new

New versions, and what changed in each. Newest first.

Version 0.2.4

Published on the Chrome Web Store on 26 October 2026

These apply to the tagged reading, and to the combined reading built on it.

  • A small figure the author described is shown as a picture. A tagged figure under 24pt on a side used to get a notice instead of its picture. If the author wrote alternative text for it, it is now shown however small it is, unless it is a thin line under 2pt. It is drawn finer than other figures so it can be made out. For these figures the picture now takes the place of the on-screen 「画像: …」 notice, and the author's words are its alternative text, as for any figure whose picture is shown. A small figure without the author's own words keeps the notice.
  • An empty alternative text is no longer treated as decorative. Since 0.2.2 the Reader took a figure with an empty /Alt to be one the author marked decorative. In what was measured, Word and PowerPoint do not mean that by it: both leave a picture marked Decorative out of the structure, and PowerPoint writes an empty /Alt for a shape given no alternative text. For such a figure the Reader now says the author gave no alternative text, as it does for one without /Alt. This reverses part of what 0.2.2 below describes.
  • PowerPoint's 「(グループ化されたオブジェクト)」 is removed from a group's alternative text, where it read as part of the author's words.

Version 0.2.3

Published on the Chrome Web Store on 25 October 2026

  • The title is read once. The Reader shows a PDF's title as the page heading and leaves out a first heading that repeats it. Word tags its Title-style paragraph as a heading, and when the title had a space between two Japanese characters the two did not match, so the title was read a second time.
  • Heading counts are the headings you can move to. The structure picker and the notice that another reading has headings no longer count a title heading the Reader does not show, and the Markdown view no longer repeats the title.
  • A tagged document whose only heading tag is its title can now get the combined reading, on the same terms as one with no heading tags.
  • Chrome 147 or later is required (Edge 147). The bundled PDF.js uses newer JavaScript without checking for it, and earlier releases already depended on it: without Math.sumPrecise, which Chrome has from 147, embedded fonts fail to load and text is read from a substitute font. That was seen in testing, not in an old browser. The store no longer installs or updates the extension in an older browser; a copy already installed there stays on 0.2.2 until the browser is updated.
  • The libraries underneath are current: PDF.js 6.4 and pdf-inspector 1.25.2, still with this project's fix for Japanese and Chinese text in fonts without a ToUnicode map. On the documents it is tested against, the structure the Reader shows is unchanged. The extension is 0.45 MB smaller installed.

Version 0.2.2

Published on the Chrome Web Store on 28 September 2026

These apply to the tagged reading, and to the combined reading built on it.

  • Each figure shows its own part of the page. Many PDFs wrap each figure in a page-sized clip — InDesign and PowerPoint exports both do — and the Reader showed the whole page in the figure's place, once for every such figure. In two documents it was checked against, that was 6 of 14 figures and 6 of 29; it is none now.
  • A figure no longer shows another figure's picture. A figure too small to crop could be matched to an image nearby. It now keeps its notice instead.
  • A picture drawn larger than its frame is shown at the size of the frame, rather than with a wide empty margin.
  • The notice under a figure without alternative text says what is known. Where the PDF could be read for it, the Reader now says the author gave none, rather than that none could be obtained; says when a figure is too small to show; and treats a figure the author marked decorative as decorative.

Version 0.2.1

Published on the Chrome Web Store on 27 September 2026

  • The analyser behind the inferred reading is a newer version of pdf-inspector. Text shown one particular way in a PDF is no longer missing, two lines of very small type set close together are no longer read as one, and a page drawn from many thin lines is analysed much faster. The fix from 0.2.0 for Japanese and Chinese text is still in it.
  • ビューアー is spelled one way in the Japanese interface.

Version 0.2.0

Published on the Chrome Web Store on 27 September 2026

  • Structure tags are read even when the PDF does not declare itself Tagged PDF. A PDF with a structure tree but without the declaration the standard requires (/MarkInfo with /Marked true) used to be read by inferring its structure from the layout. The author's tags are now used, provided they carry at least half of the text on the pages.
  • The document information panel says when a PDF does not conform. For such a PDF, "Structure tags" reads "Yes, but not declared as Tagged PDF", with a note that other viewers and assistive technology may not use its tags.
  • Japanese and Chinese text in fonts without a ToUnicode map is read. In the inferred reading and the Markdown view it came out as noise, and the page was flagged for OCR.
